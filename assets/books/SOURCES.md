## Yalnız Efe — Ömer Seyfettin (1884–1920)

This is the short-story version, not the unfinished longer novel.

Source: Turkish Wikisource, fixed revision 169450:
https://tr.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=Yaln%C4%B1z_Efe&oldid=169450

The original author's economic copyright term has expired in Turkey.
The digital transcription is attributed to Wikisource contributors and shared
under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
Paragraph whitespace is normalized for EPUB; publisher artwork and commentary
are not included. Source and license credits are also inside the EPUB.

Rebuild with `python tool/prepare_sample_book.py`.

Only Yalnız Efe is bundled. Şermin, Vatan Yahut Silistre and old-script scans
are excluded at the user's request.
