# E-reader build 11 — 2026-10-26

Includes build 10's opening, editing, information, whitespace, page-boundary and
animation changes. On phone widths of at least 300 logical pixels, current/total
page navigation and bookmark controls now share one row even with 130% system
text. The reading area gains 46 logical pixels in this configuration. Very large
navigation text can scroll horizontally within its allocated space without
displacing bookmark buttons or overflowing the reader.
